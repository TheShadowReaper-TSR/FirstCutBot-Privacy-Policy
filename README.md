# FirstCutBot-Privacy-Policy

**Last Updated:** June 2nd 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the **FirstCut Discord Bot** ("the Bot") collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the Bot, you consent to the data practices described below.

## 1. Information We Collect

Because the Bot integrates with the **FirstCut** game infrastructure and handles account creation, we collect minimal data required to make the game work:

* **Discord User ID:** A unique string of numbers associated with your Discord account. This is used as your primary identifier to link your Discord profile to your game account.
* **Discord Username & Avatar:** We may temporarily read your username or avatar to display it on in-game leaderboards or your profile.
* **Game Data:** When you create an account, we generate and store your in-game statistics, progression, and account credentials securely in the **FirstCut** game database.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly for running the game. This includes:

* Creating and authenticating your **FirstCut** game account.
* Syncing data between your Discord actions and the game's code/database.
* Displaying your progress, stats, or achievements.

## 3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

* **No Selling Data:** We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information or Discord data to third parties.
* **Service Providers:** Data is only processed through our secure database hosting providers strictly for keeping the game online.
* **Discord Compliance:** We comply entirely with Discord's Developer Terms of Service and Developer Policy.

## 4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. Because the Bot interfaces directly with game code and databases, connection strings and API tokens are heavily encrypted and never exposed to users.

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion

* We retain your data for as long as your **FirstCut** account remains active.
* If you want your account and linked Discord data permanently deleted from our system, you can submit a request on the discord.
